Page Content Scraper

Fetch a page, extract paragraphs, links, and images.

Runs entirely in your browser. Direct fetches fail on most sites due to CORS restrictions, so this app can route the request through a public proxy, trying a couple of fallbacks if the first is unavailable. Turn the proxy off if the target site allows cross-origin requests directly. Images are loaded straight from their original source URLs, so slow or hotlink-protected sites may show broken thumbnails even when the link itself is valid.
